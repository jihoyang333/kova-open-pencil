# Campaign Module: Community / Branded

Emails that humanize the brand through founder messages, team stories, mission content, and community engagement. Build long-term loyalty and reduce unsubscribes.

---

## Section Flow

```
Hero (personal photo or brand imagery with warm headline)
  ↓
Story Bridge (founder note, team spotlight, or brand narrative — 2–3 short paragraphs max)
  ↓
Visual Section (team photos, behind-the-scenes, timeline, or value graphics)
  ↓
Soft CTA ("Shop Founder's Favorites", "Meet the Team", "Learn Our Story")
  ↓
Footer
```

---

## Design Emphasis

- Personal, editorial feel — less "marketing blast", more "letter from a friend"
- Founder/team photos: real, candid, not overly produced
- Text-forward layouts work well here (20% pattern interrupt per 75/20/5 rule)
- Signature element at bottom of founder notes adds authenticity
- Brand timeline or milestone visuals for "about" content
- Warm color palette — use brand colors softly, avoid high-contrast aggressive sale styling
- Can be simpler/shorter than other types — authenticity over polish

---

## Copy Angle

- First-person or personal voice, not corporate marketing speak
- Founder letters: gratitude, personal insight, transparent journey
- Keep it genuine — readers can sense when "personal" is scripted
- Mission content: show through stories and actions, not manifestos
- CTA copy: "Shop Founder's Picks", "See Our Journey", "Join the Community", "Learn Our Story"
- Avoid being preachy — share values through what you DO, not what you believe

---

## Subtypes & Topic Ideas

1. **A Note From the Founder** — Values, gratitude, personal insight
2. **Why We Exist** — Origin story, the problem you solve
3. **Brand Timeline** — Visual journey from start to now
4. **Meet the Team** — Spotlight faces and fun facts
5. **The Story Behind [Product]** — How a specific product came to be
6. **Behind the Scenes / Launch Week** — BTS of brand operations
7. **How We're Giving Back** — Charitable partnerships, donations
8. **Our Values** — Visual explainer of core beliefs
9. **From Prototype to Product** — Evolution of your product
10. **Culture Corner** — What the team is reading/listening to
11. **We're Listening** — Feedback invite + transparency
12. **Your Stories Inspire Us** — User-submitted content + appreciation
13. **Happy [Brand] Anniversary** — Reflection + thank you
14. **Founder's Favorites** — What they personally use and love
15. **Our Vision for the Future** — Where the brand is heading
