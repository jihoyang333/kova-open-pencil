# Campaign Module: Social Proof

Emails that build credibility through customer voices, reviews, UGC, and press. Don't always convert immediately but reduce purchase hesitation for future emails.

---

## Section Flow

```
Hero (featured customer quote, result, or rating)
  ↓
Testimonial Bridge (2–3 additional testimonials or review cards)
  ↓
Product Tie-In (products mentioned in reviews, with individual CTAs)
  ↓
CTA ("Shop Best Sellers", "See Why [X]+ Customers Switched")
  ↓
Footer
```

---

## Design Emphasis

- Star ratings as visual anchors (★★★★★ with count)
- Large quotation marks or speech bubble styling for quotes
- Customer photos add credibility — circular crop, positioned with quote
- Review cards: star rating + quote + customer name + optional photo
- Bold key phrases within longer testimonials for scanners
- Pair testimonials with product images when possible (shoppable social proof)
- Trust badges and certification logos near CTAs

---

## Copy Angle

- Customer voices, not brand voice — let them sell for you
- Include customer identifiers: name + location ("Sarah M., Los Angeles")
- Specific numbers signal credibility: "Rated 4.9 by 12,400 customers" not "Customers love it"
- Stats give permission to buy: "95% reported improvement" (provide the logical alibi)
- Vary testimonial angles across emails: ease of use, product quality, results, vs competitors
- CTA copy: "Shop [Product Name]", "See Why 50,000+ Switched", "Read More Reviews"

---

## Subtypes & Topic Ideas

1. **"I Was Skeptical Until..."** — Powerful review story
2. **Before and After** — Transformation spotlight with imagery
3. **Customer Story of the Month** — Real human, real results
4. **Your Words, Not Ours** — Text-only review collage
5. **Video Review Highlight** — Feature thumbnail of customer clip
6. **Fan Favorites According to You** — Bestsellers based on reviews
7. **Top Reviewed Products** — Star ratings + mini blurbs
8. **Social Media Roundup** — UGC from Instagram/TikTok
9. **Rated 4.9 Stars... Here's Why** — Review-based breakdown
10. **As Seen In [Media]** — Press mentions and logos
11. **Real People. Real Results.** — Grid of mini testimonials with faces
12. **Influencer Spotlight** — Subtle UGC from someone with authority
13. **Customer Poll Results** — Share outcome of community vote
14. **95% of Customers Say...** — Survey-data-driven social proof
15. **Most Unexpected Reviews** — Highlight unique use cases
