# Campaign Module: Product / Collection Highlights

Emails that showcase specific products, new arrivals, trending items, bundles, or curated collections. Primary revenue driver for non-discount campaigns.

---

## Section Flow

```
Hero (product image as star, or lifestyle collection shot)
  ↓
Feature Bridge (3 key benefits with icons, or product details)
  ↓
Product Grid (2–6 items, 2 columns max, individual CTAs per product)
  ↓
General CTA ("Shop All", "View Collection", "Browse More")
  ↓
Footer
```

### Variant: Single Product Spotlight

```
Hero (dramatic product reveal)
  ↓
Feature Callouts (icon + headline + one line, 3 max)
  ↓
Social Proof Teaser (1 testimonial or rating)
  ↓
Strong CTA ("Shop [Product Name]")
  ↓
Footer
```

### Variant: Collection / Lookbook

```
Hero (lifestyle image with collection name)
  ↓
Product Grid (4–6 hero pieces, name + image + CTA each)
  ↓
Collection CTA ("Shop the Collection")
  ↓
Footer
```

---

## Design Emphasis

- Product is the hero — high-quality imagery, product shots or lifestyle in context
- Feature callouts use icons for fast scanning (3 max per section)
- Product grids: 2 columns, consistent image sizes, equal card heights
- Individual CTA per product (button or underlined name)
- General "Shop All" CTA at end of every product grid (25% click boost)
- Price optional in first view — keep it clean unless running a sale
- Consistent product photography style throughout

---

## Copy Angle

- Lead with benefit, not specs: "Sleep Better Tonight" not "Memory Foam Technology"
- Product name: 32–40px, prominent
- Feature descriptions: 1 line per feature, icon does the heavy lifting
- Collection context: "Trending This Week", "Just Landed", "Staff Favorites"
- CTA copy: "Shop [Product Name]", "Try [Product]", "View Collection", "Build Your Kit"
- Avoid generic "Shop Now" — tie CTA to the specific product or collection

---

## Subtypes & Topic Ideas

1. **Product Spotlight** — Deep-dive on one hero SKU
2. **Trending Now** — What's hot on site this week
3. **Staff Favorites** — Curated team picks with personality
4. **Just Landed: New Arrivals** — Fresh drops
5. **Bundle Builder** — Complementary product pairings
6. **Build Your Routine / Kit** — Step-by-step bundle
7. **Gift Guide** — For Her / Him / Them, by occasion
8. **Seasonal Must-Haves** — Fall, Winter, Summer essentials
9. **Back to School / Work / Gym** — Life-event themed picks
10. **Limited Edition** — Short shelf-life urgency
11. **Under $50 / Budget-Friendly** — Low-commitment AOV items
12. **Best Sellers vs Hidden Gems** — Contrast feature
13. **Editor's Picks** — High-end aesthetic curation
14. **Your Daily Essentials Kit** — Routine-builder spotlight
15. **What's Back In Stock** — High-demand restocks with urgency
