# Campaign Module: Educational

Emails that deliver value first, then bridge to product. Highest engagement rates. Build trust for future conversions.

---

## Section Flow

```
Hero (curiosity hook or pain callout)
  ↓
Educational Bridge (deliver value: 3–5 points max)
  ↓
Product Bridge (connect education to product as solution)
  ↓
Soft CTA ("Learn More", "See the Science", "Try [Product]")
  ↓
Footer
```

---

## Design Emphasis

- Infographic-style visuals: numbered lists, icon + headline + one sentence, checklists, comparison charts
- Educational blocks use icon + bold headline (2–4 words) + one sentence (under 15 words)
- Clear section breaks between educational content and product tie-in
- Product introduction feels earned, not forced — product appears AFTER education
- Soft, expert tone. No hard sell in hero.
- Longer copy budget: 100–150 words total (vs 50–100 for promotional)

---

## Copy Angle

- Open with question or surprising fact, never brand intro
- Pattern: Teach the process, sell the shortcut (Problem → Process → Solution = your product)
- Headlines that work: "What's in your [category] matters", "The [topic] connection: explained", "5 [things] your [expert] won't tell you"
- Claims compliance: "may help", "designed to", "supports", "formulated for" — no unsubstantiated guarantees
- CTA copy: "Learn More", "See the Science", "Try [Product Name]", "Start My Routine"

---

## Subtypes & Topic Ideas

1. **FAQ** — Address top 5 objections head-on
2. **Did You Know / Ingredient Spotlight** — Deep-dive one ingredient or material
3. **Problem-Solution** — Validate pain → "Here's what most people don't know" → Product as solution
4. **Mythbusters / Myths vs Facts** — Dispel misconceptions, position product as truth
5. **Tips & Tricks / How-To** — Show effective usage techniques
6. **How It's Made** — Behind-the-scenes sourcing or production
7. **What's Inside** — Ingredients, materials, what's included
8. **Step-by-Step Guide** — Visual or checklist-style usage flow
9. **The Science Behind** — Cite research, proven outcomes
10. **One Feature Focus** — Entire email about one benefit or feature
11. **What's NOT In Our Product** — Address concerns by omission
12. **Label Decoder** — Teach how to read packaging, certifications
13. **You've Been Using It Wrong** — Better usage techniques
14. **Us vs Them** — Compare your product to alternatives (use comparison chart infographic)
15. **Before You Buy** — Educate on what to expect
