# Campaign Module: Sales

Emails that drive urgency and maximize conversion through discounts, limited offers, flash sales, and special promotions. Highest immediate conversion but lowest long-term engagement.

---

## Section Flow

```
Hero (discount prominent — largest element, urgency messaging)
  ↓
Product Grid (featured products at sale prices, individual CTAs)
  ↓
Urgency Section (deadline, countdown reference, scarcity message)
  ↓
General CTA ("Shop the Sale", "Get [X]% Off Everything")
  ↓
Footer
```

### Variant: Flash Sale

```
Hero (bold discount + "Ends Tonight" / time-limited message)
  ↓
Curated Products (3–4 items, strikethrough pricing)
  ↓
Final Urgency CTA ("Last Chance", "Hours Left")
  ↓
Footer
```

### Variant: Teaser → Launch

```
Teaser Email: "Something big is coming..." (founder text style, minimal design)
  ↓
Launch Email: Clean hero with offer + product grid + deadline
```

---

## Design Emphasis

- Discount text: 48–64px, bold, unmissable (largest element in email)
- Strikethrough original price + bold sale price for clear value communication
- Discount badges on product cards (circle with "X% OFF")
- Urgency elements: "Ends Tonight", "Limited Time", "While Supplies Last"
- Product grid with individual CTAs + general "Shop Sale" at bottom
- Multiple entry points — individual product CTAs capture specific interest
- Keep it short: sale emails should be 1.5–2 screens max
- High-contrast, bold design — this is not the time for subtlety

---

## Copy Angle

- Discount/offer is the headline, not buried in body copy
- Urgency must be authentic — don't fake scarcity
- Specific discount: "25% Off Everything" not "Big Sale"
- Scarcity phrases: "This sold out in [X hours] last time", "Only [X] units left"
- CTA copy: "Get [X]% Off", "Claim Your Discount", "Save Now", "Shop Before It's Gone"
- Promotional copy budget: 50–100 words total (keep it lean)

---

## Subtypes & Topic Ideas

1. **Teaser** — "Something big is coming..." founder-style text
2. **Launch Day Sale** — Clean offer reveal with clear CTA
3. **How to Shop the Sale** — Guide-style breakdown of categories
4. **Flash Sale** — Weekend-only, tight deadline
5. **Final Hours / Ends Tonight** — Countdown urgency
6. **VIP Early Access** — Segment-only exclusive release
7. **Subscriber-Only Sale** — Email-exclusive access
8. **BOGO / Bundle Sale** — No code needed, automatic discount
9. **Holiday Sale** — Seasonal tie-in (BFCM, Valentine's, etc.)
10. **Last Call: Final Units** — Inventory countdown + scarcity
11. **Gift With Purchase** — High perceived value promo
12. **Mystery Offer** — Curiosity-driven (scratch-off style concept)
13. **Your Favorites Are On Sale** — Personalized collection framing
14. **Sale Extended** — Bonus round for lagging sales
15. **Stock Up & Save** — Tiered discount or AOV play
