09 · Canvas overlays

Visual layers Kova draws on the canvas during normal use — selection, hover, snap, frame & mask outlines, layout guides, the pixel grid, the eyedropper preview, and persistent measurement nodes. Quiet by design: industry-standard canvas conventions on Figma-faithful neutral chrome, with three Kova whispers — 4px chrome corners, neutral frame outlines, and the AI-only accent reserved for the inline assistant.
