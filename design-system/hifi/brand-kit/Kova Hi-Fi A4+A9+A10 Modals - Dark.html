FILE 5 · §6 · A9.2 Email reference

A9.2 · Email confirmation reference · scoped out

A9.2 is intentionally not designed in this batch — locked decision per the brief. The static email is rendered server-side later by the founder. The in-app surfaces (A9.1 modal + A9.3 landing) both reference the email so the user expectation is set; the email itself can be plain-text on first ship.

Why this is deferred

Designing a transactional email in the dark hi-fi vocabulary requires a separate light-mode email template (most clients render dark themes inconsistently). Rather than ship a stylized email that breaks in Outlook, we recommend the founder send a minimal plain-text message with a single restore link in MVP, and queue email-template design as its own follow-up batch when there's a second transactional surface to design alongside it.
