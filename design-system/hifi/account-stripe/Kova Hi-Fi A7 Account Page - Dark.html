Brand bible · 2026page 1 / 42 · indexed

I. Mission

Nike exists to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world. The asterisk on "athlete" — if you have a body, you are an athlete — is the foundational principle for everything we ship, write, and design.

II. Voice principles

We speak directly to the athlete. Second person. Active voice. Specific verbs. Verbs that carry effort: lace up, push off, hold the line. Avoid the language of consumer goods.

III. Visual system

Onyx and Bone are the foundation. Flame is the punctuation. Cobalt and Field are restricted to technical and outdoor categories.

  • 40/60 split chosen — sources on the left for reading-order familiarity, viewer on the right for content focus. Sources column shows the canonical KB list above and the Knowledge / Past emails / Products tab strip below (per Personalization fold-in).
